Private by design

Most online PDF sites copy your documents to their servers. Quire doesn’t: the conversion code runs inside your browser tab, and your files never leave your device.

  • On-device processingFiles are opened and saved by your browser.
  • AES-256 encryptionThe strongest standard PDF password lock.
  • No account neededNo sign-up, no email, no tracking of files.
  • Trusted enginesBuilt on Mozilla PDF.js and pdf-lib.

Frequently asked questions

Is Quire really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use with no account, no watermarks and no daily limits.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Quire runs inside your browser, so your files are opened, changed and saved on your own device. Only the Translate and AI tools send the document’s text, never the file, to a language service.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit. Very large files simply take longer, because your own computer or phone does the work.

Can I convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF?

Yes. Quire converts DOCX, XLSX, XLS, CSV and PPTX files to PDF, and PDF back to Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

Can I make a scanned PDF searchable?

Yes. OCR PDF reads the text in scans in 13 languages, including Hindi and Bengali, and makes it searchable.

Does Quire work on mobile?

Yes. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on phones, tablets and computers.
